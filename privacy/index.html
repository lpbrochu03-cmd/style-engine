Privacy

Last updated 7 August 2026

Style Engine measures how you write and shows it back to you. This page says exactly what it keeps, what it sends elsewhere, and how to get rid of all of it.

Written to be read, not to cover anyone. If something here doesn't match what the app does, the app is wrong and I want to know.

What's kept

If you never sign in and never upload anything: nothing. The Style Mirror runs inside your browser. Text you paste is measured on your own machine and is never sent anywhere.

If you upload a document: it's sent to the server, measured, and deleted immediately — the file itself is removed as soon as the numbers are computed, in the same request. What's kept is the profile: statistics about your writing (average sentence length, punctuation rates, recurring phrases) and up to eight sentences of your own writing, kept as examples of how you sound.

If you sign in: your email address, and the dates the account was created and last used. That's the whole account record. No name, no school, no age, no password — there isn't one to store.

If you use the assignment checklist: the class names, assignment titles and due dates you type in. These stay in your browser unless you're signed in, in which case they're saved to your account so they follow you to another device.

If you ask for access: your email address, whatever you wrote in the optional note, and which link brought you here.

If you subscribe: Stripe handles the payment and holds the card details. This site never sees or stores a card number. What's kept here is a license key, your email address, and the plan and status of the subscription.

What's sent elsewhere

  • Anthropic (Claude) — when you use Compose, Draft Feedback or Test Prep,

the text involved is sent to Anthropic's API to be processed, along with the measurements of your writing. This is unavoidable: those features are a model reading your text. The Style Mirror never sends anything.

  • Stripe — payment details, if you subscribe. Their privacy policy governs

what they hold.

  • Resend — your email address, to deliver sign-in links and license keys.

Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared with anyone not on that list.

What isn't done

  • No advertising trackers, no analytics scripts, no third-party cookies, no

pixels. The site loads nothing from anyone else's server.

  • Your writing isn't used to train anything, by me or anyone else.
  • Your work isn't shown to other users. Profiles are scoped to whoever created

them, and a guessed id from another session returns nothing.

IP addresses

Your IP address is used, in memory, to count requests for rate limiting so one person can't run up the bill for everyone. It isn't written to a database and it isn't linked to your account.

Getting rid of it

  • In the app: Delete it from the server removes every profile and

measurement stored for you. Signing in and deleting your account removes the account, the sessions, and everything filed under it.

  • By email: ask, and I'll delete it. There's no retention policy that

overrides you asking.

  • Deleting your browser's site data removes anything held locally, including the

assignment checklist if you aren't signed in.

Age

Style Engine is for people 13 and over. It isn't built for children under 13 and accounts aren't knowingly created for them. If you believe a younger child has signed up, email me and I'll delete the account and its data.
